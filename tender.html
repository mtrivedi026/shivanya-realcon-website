EXECUTION PLAN
Methodology for Roof Sheet Replacement

Execution Plan

Work: Replacement of roof sheet at West Coast Paper Mill Ltd.
Agency : Shivaanya Realcon Pvt Ltd. Noida

An executive plan for an aluminium shed roof replacement involves four key steps: inspecting the frame, removing the old panels safely, preparing the metal, and installing new materials.

Here is how you can organize and manage your project for a smooth replacement.

1. Preparation and Planning

  • Assess the frame: Check the metal beams for rust or damage. Replace any weak parts before installing new panels.
  • Estimate costs: Aluminium or colour-coated metal roof replacement costs typically range from depending on the material and thickness.

2. Removal and Safety

  • Gather tools: You will need a ladder, safety gloves, goggles, a drill, and heavy-duty tin snips.
  • Remove old panels: Unscrew the old aluminium panels carefully. Do not pry them, as this bends the underlying frame.
  • Safety note: Wear gloves. Aluminium edges are very sharp.

3. Installation

  • Measure and cut: Measure your shed twice. Cut the new roofing sheets to size using metal snips or a circular saw with a metal-cutting blade.
  • Overlap sheets: Lay the metal sheets overlapping by at least one corrugation (the raised bump). This stops rainwater from leaking through.
  • Fasten securely: Fix the new sheets to the frame using polymer-coated J or L-hooks. Space the hooks about 30 cm apart along the beams.
  • Use washers: Always use galvanized iron (GI) washers with bitumen (a black, waterproof material) to seal the screw holes and stop roof leaks.

4. Drainage and Maintenance

  • Slope and drainage: Ensure your roof has a natural slope. This let’s rain run off quickly so it does not pool on the metal.
  • Seal edges: Apply a waterproof sealant or flashing along the top edge where the shed meets a wall to prevent leaks.
  • Thermal expansion: Metal gets larger when it is hot. Leave small gaps at the joints and do not over-tighten screws. This gives the metal room to move.

HSE PLAN
Health, Safety & Environment Management

HSE PLAN

Work: Roof replacement work at WEST COAST PAPER LTD. Karnataka
Agency : Shivaanya Realcon Pvt. Ltd. (Noida)

A Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) plan for a roof replacement project details the rules to keep workers safe. It identifies risks like falling or electrical hazards and lists ways to stop them. The plan requires a daily safety check and strict use of gear like hard hats and safety harnesses.

1. Daily Safety Rules

  • Permit to work: Get written permission to do high-altitude work before climbing.
  • Weather check: Never work on the roof if it is raining, snowing, or very windy.
  • Tool check: Inspect ladders, ropes, and power tools before starting each day.
  • Safety talk: Hold a 5-minute team meeting every morning to discuss daily dangers.

2. Gear Requirements (PPE)

All workers must wear the following gear:

  • Hard hat: Protects from falling objects.
  • Safety boots: Slip-resistant shoes to prevent falls.
  • Safety harness: A full-body belt tied to a strong roof anchor to stop drops.
  • High-visibility vest: Helps workers see each other.
  • Hand Gloves
  • Gogels ( Chasma )
  • Life line Rope
  • Safety Net

3. Hazard Steps

  • Risk: Falls from the roof edge.
    • Fix: Install physical guardrails around the roof edge and use safety nets.
  • Risk: Falling tools.
    • Fix: Set up a "danger zone" barrier on the ground. Keep all people away from the area under the roof.
  • Risk: Cuts and scrapes.
    • Fix: Handle sharp metal or old roof pieces with thick gloves.
  • Risk: Electric shock.
    • Fix: Keep metal ladders and tools away from power lines.

4. Emergency Plan

  • First Aid: Keep a first aid kit on site. Assign at least one person trained in first aid to the team.
  • Communication: Make sure everyone has a charged phone or radio.
  • Nearest Hospital: Find the fastest route to the nearest emergency medical center before starting work.
